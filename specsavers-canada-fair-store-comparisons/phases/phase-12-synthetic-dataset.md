# Phase 12 — Synthetic Dataset and Data Dictionary

**Status:** Dataset generated and validated (synthetic) · **Prepared:** 2026-09-13 · **Revised:** repair pass (calculation engine, simplifications stated, column count corrected) · **Builds on:** Phases 1–11

> ## ⚠ Synthetic-data disclaimer
> **Every record in this dataset is fictional.** Store names, identifiers, locations, opening dates, volumes, revenue and data-quality events were **generated by a script**. They are **unrelated to actual Specsavers stores, partners, patients, performance or systems**. Province and region labels give geographic realism only; no figure represents any real location. Host banners are **generic placeholders** ("Host Banner A/B"), not real retailer names. The data must not be presented as, compared with, or used to infer real Specsavers results. No real-world events (e.g., loyalty-programme launches) are modelled.

**Derived in the model, not stored:** held appointments = booked − cancelled · unresolved statuses = booked − completed − cancelled − no-show · data-quality status (Green/Amber/Red, FR-08).

**Column count.** The Phase 12 brief listed **23** columns; this dataset has **24**: those 23 plus `weeks_since_opening`. *(The earlier text said "22 requested columns", which was a counting error.)*

**Simplifications (documented; none of these is presented as production-correct):**
- **Finalised snapshot (A-44).** Every week is treated as final, including the latest four weeks of conversion and recall and the latest 21 days of fulfilment, which production would mark provisional (KPI-05, KPI-07 to KPI-09). The dashboard banner states this. The dataset does not model late-arriving purchases or bookings, refresh times or attribution completion dates.
- **All placed orders reach "ready" (A-47).** There is no `orders_ready` column, so order-weighted turnaround uses `orders_placed` as the denominator (KPI-07 v0.2 note).
- **No negative revenue weeks.** A property of this generator run, not a business rule.
- **Completeness and refresh status are simulated** directly per store-week; they are not reconstructed from per-source load metadata (KPI-11 v0.2).
- Revenue per exam is not exam-linked (KPI-06).
- No rescheduled column (A-29); same-week re-booking of cancelled slots assumed negligible (A-28).
- **Every store is a genuinely new opening.** Converted, relocated or rebranded stores are not represented (Phase 7 TQ-12).
- No missing store-weeks after opening; the calculation engine still handles missing weeks as calendar gaps (test PY-WIN-01).

## 4. Generation method (summary)

| Component | Synthetic logic |
|---|---|
| Ramp-up | Demand factor 1 − e^(−age/16) drives utilization (≈44% at opening → ≈82% by year 2) and conversion (≈43% → ≈58%); capacity ramps from 78% to 100% of base over 20 weeks |
| Store effects | Persistent random store-level offsets for utilization and conversion; format-specific average order value |
| Cancellations / no-shows | ≈8% of booked cancelled; ≈6% of held no-show (P3 stores ≈16.5%) |
| Fulfilment | Turnaround ≈6.0 days and on-time ≈92.5% (P2 Atlantic stores ≈9.6 days and ≈74%); +0.6 days in first 13 weeks since opening |
| Recall | Not applicable before week 12 since opening; contacts grow with age; booking rate 12% / 15% / 18% by cohort |
| Data quality | Completeness ≈99.3% normally; seeded events (P5) reduce completeness, set refresh status and, where records are missing, scale received volumes down proportionally (which also creates 1–2 unresolved statuses) |
| Integrity | Counts derived top-down (slots → held → booked → cancelled / no-show → completed → purchasers) so the arithmetic always holds |

**Issues found and fixed during validation (transparency log)**
1. The first run had 6 of 6 Atlantic stores above the network median. SYN-029 was re-parameterised so one Atlantic store sits at the norm ("5 of 6").
2. SYN-005's conversion decline originally triggered its exception in the week of 2026-06-22, the **real** PC Optimum announcement date. The synthetic decline was **moved to March–May 2026** to avoid implying a link between a synthetic pattern and a real event.
3. Exception volume was high (362 flags) because of small-denominator recall noise. Recall exceptions are now limited to Developing and Mature stores with 40 or more contacts (exception rule set v0.2, Phase 13), and flags are counted by journey stage.
4. **Repair pass (calculation engine).** Four calculation defects were found and fixed in `build/kpi_engine.py`, not in the data: (a) a Red week still received a 4-week value from the three weeks before it (e.g., SYN-022 conversion 60.8% at 25 May 2026), and those values entered other stores' peer groups; (b) rates with a numerator of 1–4 were not suppressed; (c) stage grouping kept only the first triggering KPI; (d) windows were "last four rows" rather than calendar weeks. After the fix there are **228** stage flags (**8.8 per week**) and the ramp index is available for **36%** of eligible store-weeks (previously 227, 8.7 and 40%). The 32 checks still pass; the fixes are covered by `tests/test_calculations.py`.

## 6. Deliberately created patterns

### P1 — New stores improving over time
**How created:** the ramp function applies to all stores under 105 weeks since opening; three stores open inside the window.
**Evidence:** every one of the 11 stores that were New at their first row improves on both utilization and weekly exams (first 4 versus last 4 weeks), e.g.:

| Store | Weeks since opening | Utilization | Exams / week | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SYN-014 | 6 → 31 | 57.5% → 76.1% | 54 → 82 | 48.4% → 57.4% |
| SYN-030 | 0 → 22 | 49.1% → 72.0% | 36 → 68 | 40.7% → 49.6% |
| SYN-020 | 0 → 19 | 46.1% → 66.0% | 38 → 66 | 40.7% → 49.0% |
| SYN-023 | 0 → 15 | 46.4% → 65.1% | 39 → 66 | 46.2% → 51.3% |

SYN-014 crosses New → Developing in the week of 2026-07-20 (week 27 since opening), the test case for UAT-03.

### P2 — A region with elevated turnaround
**How created:** Atlantic stores (except SYN-029) have higher turnaround and lower on-time parameters.
**Evidence (periods stated):** across **all 26 weeks**, Atlantic averages **8.7 days** turnaround and **78.4%** on-time, versus **6.0 days** and **92.5%** elsewhere. In the **4 weeks ending 17 Aug 2026**: 8.9 days and 78.5% (other regions 6.0–6.1 days, 92.3–92.5%). In the **week of 17 Aug 2026** alone: 9.1 days and 77.3%. **5 of 6** Atlantic stores are above the network store median (all weeks). *Note:* SYN-030's peer-based comparison is unavailable (New-cohort peer groups under 5 stores), so rule (a) cannot flag it; its own values remain visible and the pattern shows in the regional view (UAT-06).

### P3 — High demand but lower attendance
**How created:** SYN-012 and SYN-019 have utilization ≈93.5% and no-show rate ≈16.5%.
**Evidence:** SYN-012 93.9% utilization / 83.8% attendance; SYN-019 93.6% / 83.7%; rest of network 80.2% / 93.9%. Attendance is flagged for 22 and 21 weeks respectively.

### P4 — A mature store with declining conversion
**How created:** SYN-005 (Mature, shopping centre) conversion held at ≈63% for weeks 1–6, stepped down over weeks 7–10, then drifted to ≈42%. Capacity, utilization and attendance are unchanged.
**Evidence:** conversion **62.9% → 42.9%** (weeks 1–6 versus last 8 weeks) while exams stay at **157 per week**. Rule (a) flags from 2026-04-27; rule (b) flags 2026-05-11 to 2026-06-08.

1. 761-row synthetic dataset (30 stores × 26 weeks from opening) with the 23 requested columns plus `weeks_since_opening` (24 columns).
