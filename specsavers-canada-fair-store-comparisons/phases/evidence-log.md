**Inference, not fact:** "more than 130 of more than 270 stores opened in 2025" implies that **roughly half** of the network was under about 15 months old in March 2026. Both figures are lower bounds, so the ratio is approximate; it is the author's arithmetic, not a company statement. Do not describe any public figure as "today's network".
